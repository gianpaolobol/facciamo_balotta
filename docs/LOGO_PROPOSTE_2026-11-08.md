# Dado Balotta — quattro proposte da scegliere

Richiesta approvata: dado con piu facce visibili, ognuna racconta persone/situazioni per fare balotta. Segno a inchiostro espressivo, riferimenti Pentothal/Frigidaire/Pazienza; variazioni di prospettiva e linguaggio grafico. Non cambiare il logo dell'app finche il titolare non sceglie.

- A: quasi assonometrico, tre facce equilibrate, crema/nero/rosso, conversazione/tavola/musica.
- B: prospettico e inclinato, faccia principale dominante, tratto gestuale piu ricco, rosso/lilla/giallo.
- C: tre quarti intermedio, piu sintetico e leggibile in piccolo, campiture rosso/verde e figure semplificate.
- D: vista alta a volo d'uccello, piano superiore dominante con picnic/musica, rosso/verde mare/crema.

Quattro immagini generate con lo strumento immagini integrato e mostrate nella conversazione, etichettate A/B/C/D. Sono proposte illustrative: marchio e icone attuali non sostituiti. La scelta del titolare e ancora attesa. La versione scelta andra adattata anche alla dimensione piccola dell'header e dell'icona app.

## Prompt delle proposte

### A

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished logo concept for the Italian social app Facciamo Balotta. Concept A, almost axonometric. Square cream-paper canvas #FFF5DC, one centered compact dice symbol occupying 70 percent, generous margins. A hand-drawn cube with THREE visible almost equal faces, near-axonometric construction but imperfect lively ink edges, clearly one coherent die, no numbers or conventional pips. Each face is a tiny readable comic vignette embedded INSIDE its plane: two expressive friends chatting, a person playing guitar with a friend, friends sharing a table. Faces must look like little windows onto possible social situations, not three separate stickers. Human figures, candid joyful expressions, fluid black pen line, Italian underground comics of the late 1970s/1980s, Andrea Pazienza / Pentothal / Frigidaire inspired visual language. Graphic emblem rather than detailed scenic illustration. Restricted palette dark charcoal #252923, brick red #D44935 and warm cream. Thick confident exterior outline and finer expressive interior ink, flat screen-printed color, strong recognizable silhouette suitable for app icon. No photorealism, no glossy 3D, no gradients, no long stray lines outside the cube, no lettering or wordmark. Put only a small simple letter A centered well below the emblem as a proposal identifier, separate from the logo. Original art, not existing characters.

### B

Use case: logo-brand. ONE alternative logo concept B for Facciamo Balotta, a die of possible social encounters. Square warm cream #FFF5DC background, isolated centered emblem with generous empty margins, only a small letter B below as proposal label. Make this a distinctly PERSPECTIVAL, dynamically tilted hand-drawn cube: dramatic two-point perspective, a dominant large front face, a narrower receding side face, a short foreshortened top face; coherent connected cube edges, not a flat hexagon or equal-faced isometric cube. Tilt the whole die about 10 degrees as if caught during a playful toss, without external motion lines. Replace all pips with expressive comic scenes contained within the cube's planes: large front face two friends laughing and talking face to face, side a guitarist with a companion, small top friends walking together outdoors. Human scenes rather than numbers, readable simplified anatomy. Original exuberant Italian underground comics visual language, Andrea Pazienza / Pentothal / Frigidaire inspired: gestural brush-pen strokes, lively irregular contour, expressive elongated faces and hair, selective ink hatching, black shapes with flat brick-red, dusty lilac and small warm yellow accents. More energetic and colorful than a minimal geometric emblem, but still compact and recognizable as an app brand symbol; avoid fine background detail. The narrative is that throwing this die leads to a different way of making friends. No other typography, wordmark or slogans; no glossy 3D, photorealism, gradients, conventional dice pips, disconnected panels, detached decorative outlines or random stray strokes.

### C

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE distinct proposal C for the social app Facciamo Balotta. A COMPACT GRAPHIC EMBLEM, intermediate three-quarter viewpoint between an almost axonometric cube and stronger perspective. Square warm cream #FFF5DC canvas; one centered softly rounded hand-drawn die, strong readable outline and ample margin; small standalone letter C below the die as proposal identifier, no other text. Three connected visible planes: one modest top face and two slightly unequal side faces, human irregular geometry but clearly one cube. NO dots or numbers: on each face put a very SIMPLIFIED HUMAN SITUATION drawn as bold original ink silhouettes with a few expressive facial marks. Left face: two laughing friends talking, right face: two people sharing a meal at a tiny table, upper face: two friends walking together. Make these emblem-like scenes sparse and clearly readable rather than richly detailed illustrations: maximum two people per face, no scenery, generous negative space. Inspired by the expressive brush-pen energy and wit of Italian underground comics, Andrea Pazienza / Pentothal / Frigidaire, with deliberately irregular characterful contours. Screen-print graphic solution, dark charcoal #252923 ink, cream skin/negative space, brick red #D44935 and light lime #D9E885 face colors. Broad flat color shapes, minimal hatching, no gradients, no smooth corporate stock icons. Designed to stay recognizable at small app-icon scale. A die whose different faces open onto different ways of being together. No glossy 3D, photorealism, extra ornaments, floating unrelated strokes, lettering inside the mark or conventional dice pips.

### D

Use case: logo-brand. ONE original proposal D for Facciamo Balotta, the die of social possibilities. Square warm cream paper #FFF5DC canvas. A single centered hand-inked die viewed from a HIGH BIRD'S-EYE ANGLE: the broad diamond-shaped TOP FACE must dominate about two thirds of the visible cube, with two MUCH SHALLOWER side planes visible below its front edges. Clearly different from an eye-level or equal-faced isometric cube. Slight irregular rotation and rounded edges, coherent solid cube geometry. On the large top plane show a simple comic vignette of three friends sitting in a loose circle on the grass, one with a guitar, a shared picnic; show this scene from above so guitar, heads, shoulders and shared blanket read clearly. On one small side plane two expressive laughing heads talking, on the other a pair of friends strolling. All scenes contained within their faces, replacing dice pips completely. Use playful black brush-pen drawing with the human, irreverent warmth of Italian underground comics, Andrea Pazienza / Pentothal / Frigidaire inspired; simple expressive silhouettes, bold contour, selective fine pen texture. Graphic woodcut/screen-print character with flat restrained brick red, muted sea teal and warm cream, charcoal ink; high negative-space clarity rather than dense scenery. Emblem for a social app, recognizable compact silhouette, not a detailed poster or a photoreal 3D render. No gradient, gloss, shadow background, numbers, dots, disconnected panels, stray lines outside the die or app wordmark. Only a small standalone letter D centered below the symbol, well separated, for choosing this proposal.
